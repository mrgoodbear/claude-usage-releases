# Claude Usage

A tiny always-on-top window for your Mac that shows your Claude plan limits — your 5-hour session and your
weekly limit — and when they reset. Warns you at 80% and 95%, and throws a little party when your session resets.

## Install

1. Download **Claude-Usage-<version>.zip** from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) and double-click it.
2. Drag **Claude Usage** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Open it. The first time, macOS will say it can't verify the app (it isn't from the App Store).
   Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**.
4. If you don't have Claude Code yet, a setup window walks you through it — one button, then sign in with
   your Claude account.

After that it updates itself.

## Requirements

- A Mac running macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer — Apple Silicon or Intel
- A Claude Pro or Max plan
